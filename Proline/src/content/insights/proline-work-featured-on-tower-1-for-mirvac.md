Proline is proud to have contributed to Tower 1 for Mirvac, a striking building positioned in the heart of Melbourne.

For our team, every project is an opportunity to bring the same level of care, precision and pride to the work, no matter the size or scope. Tower 1 is another example of that approach in action.

Our involvement included the in-house fabrication and installation of architectural elements delivered by the Proline team. From the work completed in our factory through to the final installation on site, the project reflects the standard we continue to build across our people, our systems and our delivery.

## Fabricated In-House

A key part of Proline's approach is our ability to bring fabrication under our own roof.

By fabricating in-house, we maintain greater control over quality, coordination and detail. It allows our team to work closely across each stage of the process, from planning and production through to installation, ensuring the finished result aligns with the standard expected on a premium commercial building.

This capability is something we continue to invest in as a business. It gives our team the ability to move with accuracy, respond quickly and take greater ownership over the work we deliver.

## Installed by Proline Hands

The installation phase is where planning, fabrication and site execution come together.

On Tower 1, the work was installed by Proline hands, with care taken to ensure each detail was delivered cleanly and professionally. Our site team plays a critical role in protecting the quality of the final outcome, and this project was no different.

Great buildings are shaped by thousands of details. The visible result is only the final stage of a process that relies on communication, coordination, craftsmanship and pride in the work.

## Pride in the Details

Tower 1 represents the type of project Proline is proud to be associated with: a strong building, a central Melbourne location and an opportunity for our team to contribute to the quality of the finished environment.

For Proline, this work reflects more than a completed scope. It reflects the way we want to operate as a business.
