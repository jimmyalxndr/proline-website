At Proline, we’ve always believed that the best results come from owning the process.

That belief has been brought to life through the delivery of the custom mesh screen package at Assemble Coburg, a project that showcases the full capabilities of our business across design, fabrication and installation.

Designed, detailed, fabricated and installed entirely under the Proline banner, this package represents a significant milestone in our journey towards becoming a true end-to-end façade and construction partner.

## A True End-to-End Solution

From the earliest concept discussions through to final installation on site, every stage of the mesh screen package was delivered by our team.

Our design and detailing teams worked closely to develop a solution that met the project’s architectural and performance requirements. From there, the screens were fabricated in-house at our Williamstown facility before being transported to site and installed by our façade team.

Bringing multiple divisions together under one roof allowed us to maintain quality, streamline communication and control every stage of the delivery process.

## The Power of Vertical Integration

For years, Proline has been working towards a simple goal: creating a business capable of delivering projects from design through to installation.

While many projects require multiple parties across different stages of delivery, this package demonstrates the advantages of having design, fabrication, logistics and installation teams working as one.

With everyone aligned around the same outcome, decisions are made faster, challenges are resolved earlier and quality is maintained from start to finish.

The result is greater certainty for our clients and a more efficient delivery process for the project team.

## Built In-House, Delivered On Site

The custom mesh screens are a visible example of the capability that continues to grow within Proline.

Fabricated locally by our team and installed by our experienced site crews, the package highlights the value of combining technical expertise with practical construction knowledge.

Seeing the screens installed on a major project is a rewarding moment for everyone involved, particularly knowing that the product has been carried through every stage of the journey by the same team.

## A Team Effort

Projects like this are never the result of one team or one individual.

From design and detailing through to fabrication, logistics, project management and installation, every department played a role in bringing the package to life.

Special recognition goes to the site team led by Riza Gashi, whose leadership and commitment helped ensure the screens were delivered safely, efficiently and to a high standard.

It’s a strong example of what can be achieved when talented people work towards a shared outcome and take ownership of the entire process.
