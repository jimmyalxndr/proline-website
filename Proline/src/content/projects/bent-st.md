Bent Street was one of our first takeover projects, involving the recladding of a non-compliant, half-finished roof. We completely removed the existing structure and designed a custom roofing and architectural system. Our team then installed the newly designed roof, showcasing our ability to innovate and deliver high-quality solutions under challenging conditions.
