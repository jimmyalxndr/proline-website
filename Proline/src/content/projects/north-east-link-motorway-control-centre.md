The North East Link Motorway Control Centre is one of Proline's most ambitious facade projects to date. Located within the Manningham compound in Balwyn North, the facility will support the operation of the twin tunnels connecting Watsonia and Bulleen.

Working alongside Kapitol Group, Proline is delivering a custom terracotta louvre system designed and modelled in-house. Detailed 3D modelling allows the team to resolve interfaces and identify potential clashes before fabrication begins, providing greater certainty for manufacturing and installation on site.

The project was first announced in October 2025, when Proline introduced the design intent and early digital modelling behind the louvre system. Since then, work has moved into an active delivery phase across a jobsite whose scale is clear from the air.

At this stage, the focus is on coordination, planning and preparing the system for delivery within a much larger infrastructure program. Bringing design knowledge into the process early helps reduce site issues, improve safety and maintain the precision required across a complex facade package.

With substantial work still ahead, the Motorway Control Centre is a major opportunity for Proline to apply its integrated design, fabrication and installation capability on one of Victoria's most significant infrastructure projects.
