Proline contributed to the new Mooroop Centre at Wantirna College, delivering facade works as part of a major upgrade to the school’s sporting and performing arts facilities.

The development includes a competition-grade two-court gymnasium alongside new music and performing arts spaces. These purpose-built environments replace aging infrastructure and provide the school community with modern facilities designed for long-term use.

Education projects require close coordination, careful detailing and a facade outcome that supports both the practical demands of the building and its architectural intent. Working with Building Engineering, the Proline team helped deliver a considered finish across a facility designed to serve the Wantirna College community for years to come.

The completed Mooroop Centre is a strong result for the school and reflects the collaboration of the full project team.
