Melton Hospital marks a major milestone in Proline’s development of modular facade delivery at scale.

Working with Lendlease, our team has spent more than 18 months drawing, designing, testing and refining a modular facade system engineered to perform financially, structurally and in demanding weather conditions, while remaining fast to manufacture and install.

The installation of the first two modular facade panels brought that work from the drawing board to the jobsite. With approximately 1,000 panels still to follow, the project is now progressing into a substantial manufacturing and installation program.

Melton Hospital reflects Proline’s continued investment in integrated design, local fabrication and site delivery. It is also an important step in our ambition to help lead the future of modular facade construction in Australia.

**Project status: In progress.**
