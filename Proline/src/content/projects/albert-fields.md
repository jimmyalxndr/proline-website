Albert Fields marks our first major contract with Mirvac, one of Australia’s largest builders. This project involves installing nearly 10,000 square meters of exterior cladding across two apartment blocks. It's a significant opportunity for us to showcase our expertise on a large scale and establish ourselves as a trusted partner for future projects with a leading developer.
