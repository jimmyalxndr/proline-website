Proline delivered the custom cladding package at 100 Franklin Street for Multiplex, contributing to the 40-level Market Way student accommodation development in Melbourne's Queen Victoria Market precinct.

The work was completed during a demanding stage of the programme, with short lead times and a clear need to keep surrounding trades moving. It required quick decisions, reliable information from site and close coordination across every part of Proline's delivery team.

Measurements captured on site were translated into detailed drawings before components moved through fabrication, powder coating and delivery. Connecting those stages within one operation allowed the team to respond quickly while retaining control over dimensions, finishes and installation sequencing.

The site and fabrication teams worked closely throughout the process, resolving details as the project developed and ensuring completed components arrived when they were needed. Safety and finish remained central despite the pace of delivery.

The result is a carefully executed cladding package delivered within a tight construction programme. 100 Franklin Street demonstrates the value of Proline's integrated approach when a project demands both speed and precision.
