Delivered for Kapitol, Yarra Valley Grammar brought together a diverse range of façade and internal cladding elements to create a highly detailed and visually striking educational environment.

The project included thousands of square metres of aluminium and COLORBOND steel cladding, custom internal linings, stainless steel features and a bespoke gold tensile mesh screen suspended within the building’s stair void. The design and installation of the mesh structure required careful planning and coordination, resulting in a dramatic architectural feature that has become a focal point of the space.

With a combination of complex detailing, premium finishes and large-scale cladding works throughout the development, the project demanded a high level of craftsmanship and collaboration across all stages of delivery. The completed outcome showcases Proline’s ability to execute architecturally ambitious projects while maintaining precision, quality and attention to detail.
