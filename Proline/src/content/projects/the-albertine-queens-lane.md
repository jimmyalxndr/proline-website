The Albertine on Queens Lane is another successfully completed project delivered by Proline for Mirvac in central Melbourne.

The project featured a large custom screen system across the rooftop, supported by a detailed package of intricate auxiliary items and custom cladding throughout the development. Each element required careful coordination, precise fabrication and a consistent approach to installation to achieve the clean architectural finish envisioned for the building.

From the prominent rooftop screening to the smaller bespoke details, the completed work reflects Proline’s ability to manage complex facade packages across multiple areas of a project. The outcome is a refined finish and another strong result delivered for one of our key clients.
