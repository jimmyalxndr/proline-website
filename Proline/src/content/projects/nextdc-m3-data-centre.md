NEXTDC M3 in Footscray is a hyperscale data centre whose sheer size is difficult to appreciate until it is seen from the air.

Proline is delivering the cladding package alongside Kapitol Group as part of the broader project team. Work on a site of this scale demands careful logistics, considered sequencing and close coordination between a large number of trades and specialists.

Every part of the delivery needs to connect with the next. From material planning and fabrication through to access and installation on site, the project reflects what can be achieved when each team takes responsibility for its part of a much larger program.

With plenty of work still ahead, NEXTDC M3 is already a powerful example of Proline’s capability to contribute to complex, large-scale data centre projects.
