Uniting Housing is a major community development completed by Proline for Hansen Yuncken in Bendigo.

Proline delivered the full facade package, with the components manufactured internally through Proline Fabrication. Keeping fabrication within our own operation gave the team close control over coordination, quality and production throughout delivery.

The scale of the building and the extent of the facade works required significant planning, precision and collaboration between our factory and site teams. The completed result is a clean, high-quality finish and another successful project delivered for the Bendigo community.

From internal manufacturing through to installation on site, Uniting Housing demonstrates the capability of the wider Proline team to deliver complete facade packages for substantial community projects.
